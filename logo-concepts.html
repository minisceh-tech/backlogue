A — Headphones + Bookmark
Current Direction
Headphones (audio/all media) frame a bookmark with a play button. The bookmark rises through the arch. Familiar, warm, communicates both tracking and playback.

B — The Stack
Collection / Library
Three fanned cards = your media collection. Depth of content at a glance. The play button on the front card says "ready to go." Works universally across all media types.

C — The Fold
Clever / Minimal
A content card with its top-right corner folded down — that fold is a play button. One shape, two meanings: bookmarked content + ready to play. Clean and ownable.

52px
D — The Arc
Bold / Ownable
A bold C-arc (referencing "Cued") with a play triangle locked into the opening. Two shapes, one unified idea. Reads instantly at any size. The most brandable option.
120px
52px
E — The Queue
Literal / Modern
Three stacked rows fading down — now playing on top, two queued below. Literally shows what the app does. The gradient fade says "there's more coming." Very readable at icon size.
